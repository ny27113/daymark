Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Daymark is a private personal planning dashboard. It stores the tasks, schedules, subjects, routines, and deadlines you choose to save in your Firebase account.

Information we use

Google Sign-In provides your email address, display name, and profile identifier so we can authenticate your account. Dashboard content is stored under your Firebase user identifier.

Sharing and retention

Your dashboard data is not public and is not sold. It remains until you delete it or request deletion from the account owner.

Security

Access is limited by Firebase Authentication, verified email-domain checks, server token verification, and Firestore ownership rules. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For account or privacy questions, use the support channel provided with your deployment.
